FAQs

The questions owners and lenders ask us most before a briefing.

What does 1iQ actually do?

It gives owners, developers and investors a plain, live answer on finish date, cost and risk for each project, and its agents handle the daily follow up so your team doesn't have to chase it.

Does it replace my GC's tools?

No. 1iQ integrates with Procore and Microsoft Project and sits above the tools your team already uses, so there is no double entry.

Does it work outside the United States?

Yes. Each site gets its own working days, weather, local news and costs, from Los Angeles to Salvador.

How does 1iQ help when there is a dispute?

Every start, issue, hold, sign off and approval is recorded with a time stamp and the name of the person responsible. When a disagreement comes up, the record is already there, so it gets settled with facts instead of a he said, she said argument. That means fewer drawn out disputes and lower legal costs.

Who is 1iQ for?

Real estate developers, family offices and private equity groups who carry the capital risk on a project and want to see it as it happens.

How is this different from a monthly GC report?

A report tells you what happened weeks ago. 1iQ shows the new finish date and what each lost day costs the morning a problem appears.

Can it help before we buy a site?

Yes. 1iQ builds the schedule, trade sequence and risk picture for a site in minutes, where a consultant study takes weeks.

How do we get started?

Book a 30 minute briefing. We walk through one of your projects and set it up together.

Does my field team need training?

The field app is built for the people on site, on iPhone and Android. It is built for the people on site, with as few steps as possible.

Who can see our numbers?

Each person sees only the projects and parts of a project their role needs. Lenders and investors get a read only Viewer seat with no draws.

Who approves schedule changes?

Your PM. Agents can suggest a change, but nothing moves a date until the PM approves it.
