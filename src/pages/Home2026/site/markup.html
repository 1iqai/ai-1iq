Sheet 12 · FAQsAsked most before a briefing

FAQs

The questions owners, developers and lenders ask us most before a briefing.

What does 1iQ do?

1iQ gives real estate developers, owners and investors a plain, live answer on finish date, cost and risk for every project, from feasibility to final draw. Its AI agents handle the daily follow up so your team doesn't have to chase it.

What is development intelligence?

Development intelligence is a live view of a real estate project built from what is actually happening on site: schedule, budget, risk and returns in one place, updated the moment work is finished or an issue comes up.

Is 1iQ construction project management software?

1iQ sits above it. Your GC and trades keep working the way they do, and 1iQ turns their progress into live schedule, cost and risk intelligence for the people who carry the capital risk.

What are 1iQ's AI agents?

Recon reminds each trade of their work every morning, Live schedule re-plans the work after every change, Site Watch flags weather, news and hazards that hit the critical path, Check work routes finished work for sign off, Pay applications builds the draw, and Live pro forma keeps returns in step with the schedule.

Does 1iQ work outside the United States?

Yes. Each site gets its own working days, weather, local news, labour and costs. 1iQ runs on projects from Los Angeles to Brazil and Nigeria.

Who is 1iQ for?

Real estate developers, family offices, private equity groups and lenders who carry the capital risk on a project, along with the owners, contractors and teams who build it.

How is this different from a monthly GC report?

A report tells you what happened weeks ago. 1iQ shows the new finish date and what each lost day costs the moment a task is finished, runs late or hits an issue.

How does 1iQ forecast the finish date and the cost of a delay?

Every time a task is finished, runs late, goes on hold or hits an issue, 1iQ re-plans the work that depends on it and shows the new finish date, the cost of each lost day and, when a pro forma is set up, the effect on interest, carry and IRR.

Can 1iQ run a construction feasibility study?

Yes. 1iQ builds the schedule, trade sequence, costs and risk picture for a site in minutes, where a consultant study usually takes weeks, and keeps it current as you change the plan.

How does 1iQ help investors judge ROI in new markets?

In markets with little reliable construction data, 1iQ combines public data with anonymised live field data from projects in that region, so you can judge returns on real local numbers instead of relying on expensive consultants.

How does 1iQ help when there is a dispute?

Every start, issue, hold, sign off and approval is recorded with a time stamp and the name of the person responsible. When a disagreement comes up, the record is already there, so it gets settled with facts instead of a he said, she said argument.

How do we get started?

Book a 30 minute briefing. We walk through one of your projects and set it up together.

How is 1iQ priced?

By project or by portfolio, scoped to what you need in your briefing.

Which tools does 1iQ connect to?

1iQ integrates with Procore and Microsoft Project, so schedules and updates flow in without double entry.

Does my field team need training?

The field app is built for the people on site, on iPhone and Android, with as few steps as possible.

Who can see our numbers?

Each person sees only the projects and parts of a project their role needs. Lenders and investors get a read only Viewer seat with no draws.

Can lenders track progress and draws?

Yes. A Viewer seat shows lenders the same live progress, schedule and budget you see, and pay applications are built from signed off work and sent as a PDF.

Who approves schedule changes?

Your PM. Agents can suggest a change, but nothing moves a planned date until the PM approves it.

Where does 1iQ's data come from?

From your project's field updates, schedule and budget, combined with public data from institutions such as the US National Weather Service, NASA, USGS, FEMA, the Federal Reserve Bank of New York, the IMF, the World Bank and the ILO, and many more global and local market sources from around the world.
